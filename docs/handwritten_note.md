# Handwritten note copy

Short on purpose. A handwritten note that runs long stops looking handwritten
and starts looking like a form letter someone traced.

Write it on a card, not letterhead. Blue ink. Do not sign it "Cleaner Greener
Future LLC."

---

## Variant A — references their usage

Use only if you are comfortable saying where the usage figure came from. A
grower who did not know his co-op data was in anyone's hands may react to this
before he reads the rest.

> [Name],
>
> Ran the numbers for a farm your size. The sheet with the table shows what it
> looks like.
>
> The other one is who we are. We are not a solar company. We find the money
> that pays for the equipment.
>
> If it looks close, send me twelve months of bills and I will get you a real
> number.
>
> Clint
> 913-349-6586

---

## Variant B — no reference to their data

Safer for a first touch. Same ask, nothing that invites the question of how you
know anything about their farm.

> [Name],
>
> Two sheets in here. One is what solar looks like on a farm your size. The
> other is who we are.
>
> We are not a solar company. We work the incentive side, so we find out what a
> project qualifies for before anyone sells you equipment.
>
> Twelve months of bills is all it takes for me to put a real number in front of
> you.
>
> Clint
> 913-349-6586

---

## Variant C — shortest

For a stack of fifty, when the note has to stay a note.

> [Name],
>
> What solar looks like on a farm your size, and who we are.
>
> Send me twelve months of bills and I will get you a real number. No cost
> either way.
>
> Clint
> 913-349-6586

---

## Why it is worded this way

**"We are not a solar company"** does the heavy lifting. It separates you from
whoever else is knocking, and it sets up the company flyer instead of repeating
it.

**One ask, both sheets.** Twelve months of bills is the only thing you want from
this envelope. It is also the thing that turns every estimate on the quote into
a real number, so the ask and the value are the same sentence.

**No deadline in the note.** The 2027 date is on both flyers already. Putting it
in the handwriting makes the note feel like a sales tool rather than a note.
